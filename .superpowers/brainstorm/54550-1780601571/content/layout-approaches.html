A — Featured + Grid

B — Accordion / Expandable List

Full-width rows, one expanded at a time showing the trainer's photo large + full credentials. Hovering/clicking expands a row. Clean, modern, no carousel. Good for 6 trainers — all visible at once.

C — Expanding Panels

Tall vertical panels side by side. Hovering one expands it wide to reveal the full portrait + bio, while others compress. Cinematic feel, great for showcasing trainer photos. Very distinctive — not a typical card grid.
